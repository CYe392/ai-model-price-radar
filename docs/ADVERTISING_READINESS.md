# Advertising activation gate

v2.2 reserves stable, visibly labeled placements and ships a site-wide privacy-choice control. It intentionally does not load a third-party advertising script.

Before activating a network, the operator must:

1. Own and verify the production domain and publish current privacy, cookie, and advertising disclosures.
2. Complete a legal review for every launch market.
3. Use a Google-certified CMP for AdSense or other Google publisher products where Google requires one, including relevant EEA, UK, and Swiss traffic.
4. Map vendor purposes into the existing necessary, analytics, and advertising choices without treating silence as consent.
5. Update the Worker content-security policy with only the exact vendor hosts required.
6. Verify that rejecting optional categories produces no ad-network or analytics request.
7. Test labeling, layout stability, mobile behavior, keyboard access, and withdrawal of consent.

## Editorial boundary

Commercial status must never enter the recommendation scoring function, price parser, data-quarantine decision, source-health state, comparison ordering, or correction workflow. A sponsored provider can receive critical coverage and cannot purchase suppression of a lower-priced route.

## Current placement behavior

`AdSlot` renders a first-party house message and records no impression. The consent attribute is available for future integration, but granting advertising consent alone does not load a network. Activation is a separate reviewed release.
